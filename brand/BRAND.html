Idea

DeadChat is a quiet place for conversations that ended. You can scroll back, listen again, and write the things you never sent. Its symbol is the single grey tick: a message that left you and never arrived. The brand should feel calm and private, a little like keeping a letter in a drawer. It's for remembering, not for social media.

The mark is an Ember speech bubble with two lines of text and one tick in the corner. It's always written lowercase: dead in Ash at normal weight, chat in Bone at semibold, so the word fades in from the dark.

Ember is the only warm colour, so use it sparingly: the mark, the main button, the active tab. Inside a chat, the bubbles keep their familiar WhatsApp colours so old conversations look the way you remember them.

The tick

you still up?2:14 am
i wrote something for you2:16 am

Messages written in DeadChat keep one grey tick forever. It isn't an error, so never colour it red. It's the honest state of the message: written, kept, and not sent.

Voice

Say
  • Never delivered.
  • Nothing leaves this computer.
  • Date not written.
Avoid
  • Exclamation marks and cheerful copy
  • “Sync”, “share”, “cloud”
  • Guessing when facts are missing

Write short, plain and gentle, and be honest about what the app can't do.
